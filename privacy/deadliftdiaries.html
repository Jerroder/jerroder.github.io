Privacy Policy for Deadlift Diaries

  1. Introduction

    Thank you for using Deadlift Diaries. I believe that you should be able to plan and track your workouts without worrying about your privacy. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how I handle your information when you use my app.

  2. Information I collect

    Deadlift Diaries does not collect, store, or share any personal data or information from its users. Our app is designed to operate entirely on your device without the need for a network connection.

  3. Local Operation and Data Handling

    • Local Processing: Deadlift Diaries operates entirely locally on your iOS device.

    • iCloud backup: if the user enables the iCloud Backup functionality, the data you have entered in the app will be sent to Apple's server and its Privacy Policy applies.

    • JSON export: Mesocycles, workouts and exercises can be exported in JSON format and uploaded to third-party websites by you. Please be aware that once this data is uploaded to these websites, their respective privacy policies will apply. Deadlift Diaries is not responsible for the privacy practices or the content of such websites.

  4. Changes to this Privacy Policy

    This Privacy Policy has no reason to change but I invite you to check this page periodically in order to see any update. The changes will be listed in the Github History.

  5. Here on Github

    This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, you can find its Privacy Policy here.
